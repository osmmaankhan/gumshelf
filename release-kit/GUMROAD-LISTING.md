# GumShelf — Gumroad listing draft

**Tagline:** Find the digital resources you already own.

## Description

GumShelf is a free, local-first Windows library for things you bought or claimed on Gumroad. If your collection has grown and you remember what you need but not its name, start with search. Browse categories and creators, save useful finds into collections, and keep hidden or archived items out of your everyday view.

The browser helper connects your signed-in Gumroad library to the app on your PC. You control scans; GumShelf does not download your purchased files. Optional AI Review is manual: preview the prepared information, choose whether to send it to a service yourself, and review the response before applying it.

Choose Light, Dark or Black for the glass-inspired interface. GumShelf is open source and independent of Gumroad. Start free, and contribute if you find it useful.

## Features

- Search-first Home and project-related text search
- Categories, creators, collections, hidden and archived views
- Optional manual AI Review
- Light, Dark and Black glass-inspired appearance
- Local library and backups

**Price:** Pay what you want ($0+); suggested $5.

**Included:** Portable Windows app folder and browser helper, with setup instructions. Source: https://github.com/osmmaankhan/gumshelf.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit; Chrome or Edge for the helper; a signed-in Gumroad account and internet connection for library scans.

## FAQ

**Is it safe?** GumShelf is designed to keep its library on your PC and has no GumShelf telemetry or remote server. The helper needs access to Gumroad pages; review the source and [privacy details](../PRIVACY.md) before installing. No software can promise zero risk.

**Does it download my files?** No. It reads library information and public product details, not purchased files.

**Is it affiliated with Gumroad?** No, it is an independent project.

**Is there a Mac version?** Not currently; this release is for Windows.
