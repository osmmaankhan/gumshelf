# Privacy

GumShelf needs no GumShelf account. It has no GumShelf telemetry or remote GumShelf server. Your library is stored on your computer, normally in GumShelf Data beside the app, or wherever GUMSHELF_DATA points. The helper communicates with the app over a local connection. Gumroad and any AI service you choose have their own privacy practices.

Local data can include product names, creators, IDs, download links, public descriptions and images, notes, collections, saved, hidden and archived choices, settings and backups. The helper stores a pairing token and connection information in your browser profile. Treat your data folder and token as private.

## Browser helper permissions

- **Gumroad domains:** read selected fields from your signed-in library list and public product pages. The list includes IDs and download links. The helper may open a purchase/download page to find its public storefront link; it does not extract receipt or purchase-page text or license keys.
- **Localhost (127.0.0.1):** exchange jobs and results with the app on your PC.
- **Scripting:** read relevant page fields during a scan.
- **Storage:** remember pairing and connection state in your browser profile.
- **Alarms:** periodically check the connection or for work.

The helper may use background tabs during a scan. It does not download purchased files. Gumroad receives requests from your signed-in browser, and image hosts may receive artwork requests.

## Optional AI Review

AI Review is user-initiated and manual. Preview the export, decide whether to submit it to an external AI service yourself, then review its response before applying it. Hidden products are excluded. The export includes selected product details and public storefront URLs; only verified public descriptions are included, not old purchase-page text. Redaction removes recognized sensitive patterns, emails and links from text, but automatic filters cannot guarantee they catch everything. Check the preview before sharing; your chosen service receives what you submit.

Back up your data folder with the app closed. Remove private details from screenshots, exports and issue reports before sharing.
